## 3. Data categories and sources

We process the following categories as needed to provide the app:

- **Content supplied by users:** project names, lesson titles and descriptions, root causes, recommendations, categories, phases, impact, tags, project context, and watchlist notes.
- **Account and responsibility information:** Atlassian account identifiers for creators, authors, owners, and recorded changes. Profile display names and avatar URLs are retrieved from Confluence to display people and enable owner selection.
- **Confluence references:** source and home page identifiers, macro identifiers, and accessible page titles, links, labels, and page-operation information used for permission checks and context suggestions.
- **Activity metadata:** creation and update times, latest update/deletion attribution where recorded, watchlist statuses and dates, first Actioned history, logical removal markers, and project-specific Not relevant choices.
- **Diagnostics and support:** operational error information and information you choose to send in support correspondence. Atlassian may collect additional platform-level operational data under its own arrangements.

Application records originate from users and Confluence APIs. Support information originates from correspondence. Profile names and avatar URLs are retrieved for display rather than stored as a separate application profile directory in the reviewed database schema.

Do not submit credentials, special-category personal data, or content that is unsuitable for sharing across your Confluence site. Free-text fields may contain personal information if users enter it; avoid unnecessary personal detail.

## 4. Purposes

Data is used to maintain shared projects; capture and display lessons; search and match lessons; manage watchlists; show attribution and responsibility; apply write permissions; calculate completeness and reuse statistics; diagnose failures; and respond to support, privacy, or security requests.

No separate advertising, third-party analytics, or AI integration was identified in the reviewed app source. The publisher must reassess this statement for each release and separately account for support and infrastructure providers.

## 7. Retention, deletion, and uninstalling

In the current implementation, deleting a lesson marks the record deleted, and removing a watchlist item marks the relationship removed. The records are not immediately permanently erased. First Actioned history is retained for reuse reporting. Removing a macro or source page does not establish permanent deletion of app records.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the relevant controller's role, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, receive portable data, object to processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. These rights are subject to their legal conditions and exceptions.

Contact your organization's administrator for requests concerning content it controls. You can also email Sofrato, which will identify the responsible party, verify requests as appropriate, and coordinate handling under applicable law and agreements. Do not send identity documents or full data exports in an initial request. You may complain to the competent data protection supervisory authority.

Providing account information and required lesson fields is necessary to use the corresponding app features. Declining to provide them may prevent those features from working. Discuss alternative working arrangements with your organization if the app's sharing model is unsuitable.
