# Support

Contact Sofrato at **[contact@sofrato.com](mailto:contact@sofrato.com)** for installation questions, app problems, feedback, or data requests.

## Product and installation support

For Confluence account access, page permissions, or your organization's installation approval, first contact your own Confluence administrator. For application behavior or an installation link, email Sofrato.

Describe:

1. The area: installation, Add Lessons, Discover, Watchlist, or Hub.
2. The steps that led to the issue.
3. What you expected and what happened instead.
4. The approximate date, time, and time zone, and any visible error text.
5. Whether the issue affects one user, one project, or several people.

A sanitized screenshot can help. Provide your site address privately only when needed for diagnosis. Never send passwords, API tokens, or session cookies. Share lesson text, account identifiers, and logs only when necessary and appropriately redacted.

## Privacy and security

Use the same email with subject **Privacy request — Lessons Learned Hub** for a personal-data request, or **Security report — Lessons Learned Hub** for a suspected vulnerability or exposure. Security reports should be sent privately, not in public issues.

For a data request, identify the relevant organization or site and the request without sending a full data dump. The publisher may need to verify identity and authority and coordinate with your organization's administrator.

## Service expectations

No contractual response-time or availability SLA is included in this documentation. Any separate agreed support commitments and applicable legal deadlines remain unaffected. The app is not an emergency reporting channel.
