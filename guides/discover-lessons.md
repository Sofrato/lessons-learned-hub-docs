# Discover lessons

Find relevant experience from other projects in **Use Lessons → Discover**.

## Set the project context

You need project management permission: you created the project or can edit its home page.

1. Open the Use Lessons macro for your project and select **Discover**.
2. Choose **Set project context**, or **Edit context** when context already exists.
3. Add relevant **Tags**, select **Categories**, and optionally a **Phase**.
4. You may use the page-label suggestions where available. Check that they describe the project.
5. Choose **Save context**.

At least one tag, category, or phase is required to save context. For meaningful recommendations, use a shared tag or combine a category with a phase; a category alone or a phase alone does not meet the match threshold.

## Review suggestions

Expand a lesson to read its problem, root cause, recommendation, owner, and source link. The match explanation shows which tags, category, or phase match your context.

- **Good match:** score 2–3.
- **Strong match:** score 4 or more.
- An **Incomplete** lesson can still be suggested. Ask its owner for context when needed; the app does not send that message for you.

Matching is based on shared tags, category, and phase. It is a rule-based recommendation, not a guarantee that the lesson applies to your project. See [Matching reference](../reference.md#discover-matching).

## Add a lesson to your watchlist

1. Expand a useful suggestion.
2. Choose **Add to watchlist**.
3. Open **Watchlist** to record your action, owner, and review date.

The lesson is no longer shown in Discover while it is on that project's active watchlist, regardless of its watchlist status. This does not copy or transfer the original lesson.

## Mark a suggestion as Not relevant

Choose **Not relevant** to hide a suggestion for the current project. Use **Undo** in the confirmation message if you made a mistake. The app shows a count of hidden suggestions; the current interface does not provide a general hidden-lessons management screen.

This action affects the project's recommendations, not just your personal view. It does not delete the lesson or hide it from other projects or the Hub.

## If suggestions are empty

Check that other projects contain lessons with matching tags or matching category and phase. Your own project's lessons, logically deleted lessons, lessons marked Not relevant for your project, and lessons already on its watchlist are excluded.

Discover returns up to **50** suggestions, ranked from a candidate set of up to **500 recent matching lessons**. Use the Hub for broader searches. Changing context does not clear existing Not relevant choices.
