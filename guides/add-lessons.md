# Add Lessons

Capture a useful lesson while the experience is fresh, then complete it when more information is available.

## Before you start

Use an **Add Lessons** macro linked to the correct project. You need permission to edit its page to create a lesson. Lessons are shared across the site, including when their source page is restricted.

## Create a lesson

1. Open the page containing the project's Add Lessons macro.
2. Choose **Add Lesson**.
3. Enter **Title**, **Problem description**, and **Owner**. These fields are required.
4. Add **Root cause**, **Recommendation**, **Category**, **Phase**, **Impact**, and **Tags** if available.
5. Check that the wording is suitable for everyone on the site.
6. Choose **Save lesson**.

**Expected result:** the saved lesson appears in the project's list and in the Hub. The author and source page are recorded automatically.

Keep the title specific, describe what happened without attributing blame, and make the recommendation something another team can act on. Do not enter passwords, tokens, or information that is unsuitable for site-wide sharing.

## Complete a lesson later

You can save with the three required fields even when the remaining fields are missing. The app marks the lesson **Incomplete** and identifies the gaps.

1. Expand the lesson in Add Lessons, or open it in the Hub.
2. Use **Edit** or the available **Fill in** action.
3. Complete the missing fields and choose **Save lesson**.

A lesson becomes **Complete** when it has root cause, recommendation, category, phase, impact, and at least one tag, in addition to the required fields. This is a completeness check, not an approval or quality review.

## Find a lesson

Use the macro's search and filters. Clear filters if a lesson seems to be missing, and use the page controls when the result list has several pages. The [Hub](hub.md) searches across projects.

## Edit an existing lesson

You need edit permission on the lesson's **source page**, even when you open it from a different macro or from the Hub. Being the author or assigned owner alone does not grant editing permission.

Choose **Edit**, update the fields, and choose **Save lesson**. The current record stores who last updated it and when; this is not a full version history with a restore interface.

## Delete a lesson

1. Open an Add Lessons macro for the lesson's project and find the lesson.
2. Expand it and choose **Delete**.
3. Review **Delete this lesson?**, then choose **Delete** again to confirm, or **Cancel**.

The action is available to its author or a person who can edit its source page. The lesson disappears from normal app views. **Delete is a logical deletion, not immediate permanent data erasure.** There is no documented user-facing restore action for deleted lessons. Contact support about erasure requests.

For field limits and available values, see [Reference](../reference.md).
